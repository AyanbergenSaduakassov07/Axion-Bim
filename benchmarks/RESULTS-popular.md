## Notes (2026-11-11)

Models: the sample files most often used to test IFC tools: the Revit ARC/STR/MEP and SampleHouse models
from the buildingSMART certification datasets, plus the Duplex and SampleCastle samples
(github.com/youshengCode/IfcSampleFiles). They are not committed; licenses vary (docs/release-check.md row 14).

- **No crashes.** 8 models, 16,279 elements, IFC2X3 and IFC4, 2 MB to 49 MB. The slowest run (Revit MEP, 6,824
  elements, 6,689 warnings) finishes in 14.6 s, including a BCF with one topic per open finding.
  The Revit ARC pair peaks at 2.7 GB RSS (geometry of large Revit walls), below the 8 GB job limit.
- **The fix is detected.** Revit_ARC → Revit_ARC_FireRatingAdded is a real buildingSMART revision pair where
  the architect added `FireRating` to the walls. With the IDS, fails drop 49 → 7 and MHI rises 82.1 → 97.9.
  In the web app the comparison lists 42 IDS findings as `resolved`. The 5 walls the architect missed
  stay `persistent`.
- **Real export defects, correctly reported.** The Revit ARC walls carry `NetVolume` twice: once in mm³
  (8.4 × 10⁹) although the file declares m³, once correctly (4.52 m³). QTO-002 flags the wall as
  "declared volume disagrees with geometry" and shows both values in the evidence. Column
  `OuterSurfaceArea` has the same mm²-as-m² defect. Axiom has no area check yet, so it is shown, not flagged.
- **SampleHouse is rejected** (MHI 74.7 < 80): a small hand-built IFC4 file with sparse properties.
- **SampleCastle** has the same content as Schependomlaan (RESULTS-heavy.md) under another name; same results.
- **Duplex_Plumbing / Clinic_HVAC MHI 100 with warnings:** warnings (missing classification) count toward
  coverage, not the score (D-15).
